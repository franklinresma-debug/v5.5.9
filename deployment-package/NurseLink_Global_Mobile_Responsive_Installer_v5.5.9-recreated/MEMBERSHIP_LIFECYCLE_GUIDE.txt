NurseLink v5.2.0 Membership Lifecycle Guide
=============================================

CONCEPT
-------
Application approval is permanent historical information.

Professional membership standing determines current NurseLink member-service
access.

Approved membership is therefore not overwritten when a member is suspended
or becomes inactive.

STANDINGS
---------
ACTIVE
Member is in current active NurseLink professional standing.
Member-only services are available.

SUSPENDED
Membership remains approved and member number remains permanent.
Member-only services are temporarily unavailable.
Digital ID and public verification show Suspended.

INACTIVE
Membership remains approved and member number remains permanent.
Member-only services are unavailable.
Digital ID and public verification show Inactive.

ADMINISTRATION
--------------
Use:
https://app.amsertech.com/nurselink-member-registry.html

Select an approved member and use Membership Lifecycle Management.

Every change requires a written reason.

ADMINISTRATOR
-------------
Can manage other members' standing.
Cannot manage their own standing.

SUPER ADMINISTRATOR
-------------------
Can manage standing.
Own-membership standing changes require explicit self-action confirmation and
are written to the audit trail.

AUDIT
-----
Standard:
membership.standing_changed

Super Administrator self-action:
membership.standing_self_action_super_admin

NOTIFICATIONS
-------------
Members receive notifications when standing changes to:
- Active
- Suspended
- Inactive

ACCESS ENFORCEMENT
------------------
EnsureApprovedNurseLinkMember now requires:
  membership.status = approved
  AND
  membership.standing = active

Super Administrator Test Mode remains a short-lived QA exception requiring
both Super Administrator authority and separate Administrator Portal elevation.

PUBLIC PROFILE
--------------
The public professional profile and its photo endpoint require Active standing.

PUBLIC VERIFICATION
-------------------
Verification continues to recognize the permanent approved membership record,
but reports the current standing. It does not expose internal standing reasons.

DATA RETENTION
--------------
Suspension or inactivity does not delete:
- member number
- approval date
- professional profile records
- credentials
- employment history
- learning records
- portfolio records
- audit history

REACTIVATION
------------
Suspended → Active
Inactive → Active

Reactivation requires a reason and is audited.
