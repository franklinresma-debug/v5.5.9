NurseLink v5.5.2 Credential Renewal & Compliance Guide
=======================================================

MEMBER WORKFLOW
---------------
1. Open Credential Renewal Center.
2. Find an expiring or expired credential.
3. Start Renewal Plan.
4. Set a target date and optional notes/reference.
5. Move the NurseLink workflow through:
   Planning -> In Progress -> Submitted.
6. If Administrator returns the workflow, revise it and resubmit.
7. When the NurseLink workflow is completed, update the actual credential
   record separately with the new issue/expiry details.
8. Credential verification remains a separate reviewer-controlled process.

ADMINISTRATOR WORKFLOW
----------------------
1. Sign in through the separate Administrator Portal.
2. Open Credential Compliance Center.
3. Review expiry risk and workflow status.
4. Filter/search the workload.
5. Manage an existing member renewal workflow.
6. Use Returned when the NurseLink workflow needs member action.
7. Use Completed only when the internal workflow is finished.
8. Do not treat Completed as external regulator certification.

PRIVACY
-------
The Compliance Center excludes:
- credential numbers
- uploaded documents
- reviewer notes
- home address
- phone number

ALERTS
------
The included alert utility can create NurseLink notifications for active
approved members with credentials due within 180/90/30 days or already expired.

Dry run first:
php nurselink_credential_alerts.php /home/frankresma/nurselink-api --dry-run

No cron is installed automatically.

GOVERNANCE
----------
NurseLink expiry intelligence and workflow states are advisory/internal.
Official validity, renewal eligibility, fees, appointments, regulatory
documents and final issuance remain with the issuing body or regulator.
