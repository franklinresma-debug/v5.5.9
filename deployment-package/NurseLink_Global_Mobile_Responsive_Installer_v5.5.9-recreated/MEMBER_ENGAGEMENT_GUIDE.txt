NurseLink v5.5.2 Member Engagement Guide
=========================================

MEMBER FLOW
-----------
1. Open Member Engagement Hub.
2. Review chapter, event and mentoring participation.
3. Follow useful recommended actions.
4. Join a chapter if relevant.
5. Register for NurseLink activities.
6. Configure mentoring only if desired.
7. Use the hub as an internal NurseLink participation summary.

ADMIN FLOW
----------
1. Sign in through the separate Administrator Portal.
2. Open Engagement Command Center.
3. Review aggregate member engagement workload.
4. Use Event Management for activities and attendance.
5. Use Chapter Management for chapters and member requests.
6. Use the Super Administrator Test Center for QA.

PRIVACY
-------
The Engagement Command Center is aggregate-only.
Private mentoring messages and private contact information are excluded.

GOVERNANCE
----------
Participation metrics and mentoring roles are community/productivity features.
They must not be represented as official regulator, licensing, employer or CPD
credentials.
