NurseLink v5.1.0 Membership Administration Guide
==================================================

Administrator Sign In:
https://app.amsertech.com/nurselink-admin-login.html

Membership Administration:
https://app.amsertech.com/nurselink-membership-administration.html

Core roles:
Reviewer            Review and prepare applications.
Administrator       Final decisions, review assignment, member standing.
Super Administrator All Administrator functions plus privileged role changes.

Recommended operating workflow:
1. Review Submitted applications.
2. Assign a reviewer, priority and review due date.
3. Move the application to Under Review.
4. Request information when needed.
5. Move complete applications to Ready for Approval.
6. An Administrator performs final Approve or Decline.
7. Approved applications receive the existing permanent NurseLink member
   number and Active membership standing.
8. Use Member Lifecycle for later suspension, inactivity or reactivation.
9. Super Administrators manage staff roles from Admin Roles & Staff.
10. Use Audit Activity for accountability and review of administrative actions.

Security boundaries:
- Separate Administrator sign-in is mandatory.
- Reviewer cannot make final membership decisions.
- Administrator cannot grant privileged roles.
- Super Administrator is required for role grants/changes/revocation.
- Last-Super-Administrator and self-revocation protections remain active.
