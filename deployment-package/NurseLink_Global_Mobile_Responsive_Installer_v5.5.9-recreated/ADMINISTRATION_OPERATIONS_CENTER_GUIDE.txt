NurseLink Administration Operations Center Guide — v5.3.0
=============================================================

Administrator Login:
https://app.amsertech.com/nurselink-admin-login.html

Administration Operations Center:
https://app.amsertech.com/nurselink-admin-dashboard.html

Dashboard
  Cross-platform operational metrics and attention queues.

Members
  Approved-member registry, standing and post-approval onboarding.

Applications
  Membership review, reviewer assignment and approval workflow.

Verification
  Professional credential review and verification notes.

Organizations
  Institutional and employer/recruitment partner administration.

Programs
  Chapters, benefits, engagement and enterprise programs.

Employment & Opportunities
  Workforce opportunities and member job-application review.

Training & Events
  Events, attendance/program management and credential compliance.

Communications
  Targeted in-app member notifications with controlled audit metadata.

Reports & Analytics
  Institutional/platform aggregate reporting.

Support Cases
  Case ownership, status, priority and resolution workflows.

Audit Logs
  Normalized administrative events; raw database snapshots are not shown.

System Health
  Release, required tables, storage and readiness.

Settings
  Portal governance and privileged staff access.

Maintenance rule:
New Administrator capabilities should be added as governed workflows inside
these operational domains whenever practical. Do not make raw database editing
the normal Administration interface.
