NurseLink Administrator Portal Guide — v5.2.0
================================================

ONE ADMINISTRATOR SIGN-IN
-------------------------
https://app.amsertech.com/nurselink-admin-login.html

ONE PRIMARY ADMINISTRATOR PORTAL
--------------------------------
https://app.amsertech.com/nurselink-admin-dashboard.html

The consolidated portal contains:
- Overview
- Membership
- Onboarding
- Members
- Roles & Access
- Programs
- Enterprise
- Operations

ROLE MODEL
----------
Reviewer:
- membership review preparation
- allowed non-final review transitions
- reviewer notes

Administrator:
- Reviewer capabilities
- final membership approval/decline
- review assignment
- member standing
- onboarding administration
- Administrator-level modules

Super Administrator:
- Administrator capabilities
- privileged role grants/changes/revocation
- protected high-privilege self-actions
- Super Administrator test/operations functions

SAFETY
------
- Separate Administrator sign-in remains mandatory.
- Normal Member sign-in does not establish an Administrator session.
- Final membership decisions remain role-gated.
- Ready-for-Approval remains required for final approval.
- Self-revocation is blocked.
- The last active Super Administrator is protected.
- Audit logging remains authoritative.

ADVANCED MODULES
----------------
Some specialist Program, Enterprise and Operations screens remain standalone
advanced modules for now, but they are launched from the Administrator Portal
and use the same privileged session. They are not separate login experiences.
