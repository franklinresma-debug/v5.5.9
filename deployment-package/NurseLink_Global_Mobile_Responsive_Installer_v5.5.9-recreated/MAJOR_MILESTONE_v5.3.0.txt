ARCHITECTURE
------------
NurseLink administration is workflow-first. Administrators use governed
NurseLink controllers and operational services rather than direct raw
production database editing.

The Operations Center consolidates membership, workforce, organizations,
programs, communications, analytics, support, audit and system-health
workflows under the same separate Administrator session.

NEW SUPPORT CASE FOUNDATION
---------------------------
Migration:
2026_08_14_040000_create_nurselink_support_cases_table.php

Table:
nurselink_support_cases

Support Cases include case number, optional member/organization, category,
priority, status, subject, description, Administrator owner, internal notes,
resolution summary, and activity/resolution/closure timestamps.

User-ID columns adapt to the live users.id schema, including UUID-style
char(36) production IDs.

GOVERNANCE
----------
- raw database administration is not the normal Administrator interface
- message body is excluded from communication audit metadata
- normalized Audit Logs do not return raw before_state / after_state snapshots
- database credentials and raw environment secrets are not exposed
- privileged role changes remain Super Administrator-only
- self-revocation and last-Super-Administrator protections remain enabled
- final membership approval remains role-gated
- Ready-for-Approval remains required before approval
