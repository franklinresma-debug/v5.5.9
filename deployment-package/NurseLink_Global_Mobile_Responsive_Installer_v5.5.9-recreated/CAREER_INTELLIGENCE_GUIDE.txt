NurseLink v5.5.2 Career Intelligence Guide
==========================================

Career Intelligence URL:
https://app.amsertech.com/nurselink-career-intelligence.html

Who can access it:
Approved NurseLink members only.

What the overall score measures:
- Career Profile: 20 points
- Credentials: 20 points
- Professional Experience: 20 points
- Learning & Development: 15 points
- Mobility Readiness: 15 points
- Market Alignment: 10 points
Total: 100 points

Readiness bands:
85–100  Career Ready
70–84   Strong Foundation
50–69   Developing
0–49    Build Your Foundation

Credential expiry forecast:
NurseLink reviews expiry dates recorded in the Credential Registry and surfaces:
- expired
- expiring within 90 days
- renewal window within 180 days

Always confirm renewal rules directly with the issuing/licensing authority.

Learning recommendations:
Recommendations are inferred from:
- stated specialty interests
- recorded employment specialties
- completed NurseLink learning records

They are developmental suggestions only. They are NOT official CPD requirements.

Mobility readiness:
If overseas work is selected as a career preference, NurseLink reviews:
- target destinations
- current verified credentials recorded in NurseLink
- overseas employment history
- availability date
- relocation preference

This is NOT visa, immigration, deployment, foreign-license or recruitment clearance.

Explainable job fit:
Career Intelligence reuses NurseLink’s explainable job matching model:
Role                25
Specialty           20
Target location     15
Work setting        10
Employment type     10
License/profile     10
Experience           5
Overseas preference  5

Job-match scores organize opportunities only. They do not guarantee hiring,
interviews, licensing, visa approval or employment.

Readiness history:
Members may save a Career Intelligence snapshot. Up to 24 recent snapshots
are displayed so a nurse can see how their profile develops over time.

Privacy:
Career Intelligence is member-only. It uses the member's own NurseLink data.
No Career Intelligence endpoint is public.

Recommended workflow:
1. Complete Career Matching Profile.
2. Keep Credential Registry current.
3. Keep Employment / OFW history current.
4. Record completed professional learning.
5. Review Career Intelligence priority actions.
6. Save a snapshot after meaningful profile updates.
7. Review job-fit explanations before applying.
