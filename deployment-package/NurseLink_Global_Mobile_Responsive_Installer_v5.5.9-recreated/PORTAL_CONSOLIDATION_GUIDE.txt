MAINTENANCE RULE GOING FORWARD
------------------------------
1. Do not create a new login page for a feature.
2. Member functions belong under /dashboard whenever practical.
3. Privileged staff functions belong under the Administrator Portal.
4. Reviewer/Admin/Super Admin are roles inside one Administrator Portal.
5. A new standalone page should be an advanced module only when consolidation
   would make the primary portal materially harder to maintain.
6. Old URLs should redirect into a portal section when they are superseded.
7. Authentication, role checks, navigation, and portal entry points should be
   centralized rather than duplicated.
