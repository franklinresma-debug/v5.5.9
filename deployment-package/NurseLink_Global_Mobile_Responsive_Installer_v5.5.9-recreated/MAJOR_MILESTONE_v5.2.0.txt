NurseLink v5.2.0 Major Milestone — Portal Consolidation & Maintainability
=========================================================================

OBJECTIVE
---------
Reduce the number of primary entry points and duplicated membership/admin UI
surfaces without discarding the working NurseLink backend, audit controls,
membership rules, or advanced program modules.

PRIMARY ENTRY POINTS
--------------------
MEMBERS / APPLICANTS
Sign in:
https://app.amsertech.com/login

Portal:
https://app.amsertech.com/dashboard

ADMINISTRATORS / REVIEWERS / SUPER ADMINISTRATORS
Sign in:
https://app.amsertech.com/nurselink-admin-login.html

Portal:
https://app.amsertech.com/nurselink-admin-dashboard.html

There are now two primary sign-in experiences only: Member/Applicant and
Administrator.

CONSOLIDATED ADMINISTRATOR PORTAL
---------------------------------
The Administrator Portal contains one persistent navigation with:

- Overview
- Membership
- Onboarding
- Members
- Roles & Access
- Programs
- Enterprise
- Operations

Core membership workflows now run directly inside the Administrator Portal:
- pending membership review queue
- reviewer assignment
- priority and due-date management
- membership review transitions
- final approval / decline using existing role gates
- post-approval onboarding management
- member registry and membership standing
- Reviewer / Administrator / Super Administrator access management

Advanced program, enterprise and operations modules remain available behind
the same Administrator session. They are no longer presented as separate
sign-in destinations.

CONSOLIDATED MEMBER PORTAL
--------------------------
The existing React /dashboard remains the Member Portal.

For Approved NurseLink members, v5.2.0 adds Membership & Activation directly
to the dashboard:
- member number and standing
- onboarding status
- activation score
- orientation completion
- professional profile readiness
- credential readiness
- portfolio readiness
- NurseLink digital member identity readiness

The normal /login page also exposes the single Administrator sign-in choice,
rather than directing staff through feature-specific links.

BACKWARD COMPATIBILITY
----------------------
Existing bookmarks continue to work, but key membership URLs are now
compatibility redirects:

/nurselink-membership-command-center.html
  -> /nurselink-admin-dashboard.html#membership

/nurselink-membership-administration.html
  -> /nurselink-admin-dashboard.html#membership

/nurselink-membership-onboarding-admin.html
  -> /nurselink-admin-dashboard.html#onboarding

/nurselink-member-registry.html
  -> /nurselink-admin-dashboard.html#members

/nurselink-membership-welcome.html
  -> /dashboard#membership

This preserves old links while removing those pages as primary maintained
workspaces.

MAINTAINABILITY
---------------
New shared configuration:
nurselink-portal-config.js

New consolidated Administrator styles:
nurselink-admin-consolidated.css

The central configuration owns:
- Member login / portal entry
- Administrator login / portal entry
- Administrator section definitions
- compatibility redirects
- advanced Administrator module grouping

SECURITY
--------
Existing security boundaries remain authoritative:
- separate elevated Administrator session
- Reviewer vs Administrator vs Super Administrator capabilities
- Ready-for-Approval gate
- final approval/decline Administrator gate
- Super Administrator-only privileged role management
- self-revocation protection
- last-Super-Administrator protection
- membership lifecycle audit trail
- onboarding privacy boundaries

No government, PRC, regulatory or official CPD authority is claimed.
